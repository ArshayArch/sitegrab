What you actually get

Most "free site plan" tools online give you a flat screenshot or a rough massing block. SiteGrab generates a real, usable model built from actual data for the location you enter:

1

Real building geometry, not guesses

Footprints and heights are pulled from open mapping data, with real LiDAR-derived heights where available, so neighbouring buildings sit at their actual scale.

2

Aligned and projected correctly

SiteGrab auto-detects the correct UTM projection for your location, so your model lands true-to-scale and correctly oriented — no manual georeferencing.

3

Sun path included

Every site plan can include a sun path diagram for the exact coordinates you entered — hourly sun positions and shadow casting, generated alongside your model, not as a separate manual step.

4

Export straight into your workflow

Download as a 3D Rhino .3dm file, a layered AutoCAD .dxf, or both combined — ready to drop into your existing project.

Frequently asked

Is it actually free?

Yes — you can generate site plans on the free tier. A paid plan exists for higher usage and additional features, but you don't need it to try SiteGrab or produce a working model.

What file formats do I get?

3D Rhino .3dm, 2D AutoCAD .dxf with organised layers, or a combined aligned model with both.

Does it work anywhere in the world?

Yes, anywhere with available open mapping data. Building height accuracy is highest in regions with LiDAR coverage, with type-based estimation used elsewhere.

Do I need QGIS or GIS experience?

No. SiteGrab is built specifically so you don't need GIS software or experience to get a usable, correctly scaled site model.
